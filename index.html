About

I’m an incoming PhD student at Khoury College of Computer Science at Northeastern University, joining in Fall 2026. I previously completed a Master's degree in Computational Linguistics at the University of Washington, advised by Dr. Fei Xia. Before that, I earned dual bachelor’s degrees in Psychology and Data Science from the University of Rochester. My research focuses on building and evaluating NLP systems that are reliable, interpretable, and socially responsible. Outside of research, I enjoy sci-fi novels, films, and time with my cat.
